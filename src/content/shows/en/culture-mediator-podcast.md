This is the first episode of my personal show.

## The question

How does cultural work move from specialist circles into public life?

What roles do editors, hosts, critics and content creators play in that journey?

## In this episode

- the basic role of cultural intermediaries;
- the tension between professional judgement and the attention economy;
- why audiences still need reliable ways to discover and assess content;
- how cultural creators can build trust over time.
